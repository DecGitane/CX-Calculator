VFD operating point

Assumes standard V/Hz: constant torque up to base frequency, then constant power (field weakening). Speed uses rated slip. Self-cooled motors lose cooling at low speed, so check the motor's derating curve before running at low frequency for long periods.

Power · torque · speed

Power & current

Voltage drop

ΔV = Vc × I × L / 1000. Use the 3-phase or 1-phase Vc for the cable from the AS/NZS 3008.1 voltage-drop tables or the cable maker's data.

Insulation resistance

IEEE 43 recommends PI ≥ 2.0 for class B, F and H windings. PI isn't meaningful when the 1-min reading is above about 5000 MΩ. Disconnect VFDs and electronics before testing.

Affinity laws

Q ∝ n, H ∝ n², P ∝ n³. These hold on the system curve with no static head. With high static head, flow drops off much faster than the speed ratio.

Pump power

Wet-well drawdown test

Measure inflow (rise test)
Pump flow = area × drop / time + inflow. Use a level band with straight walls (no benching) and time at least 60 s for accuracy.

Pipe velocity

Rising mains for sewage are typically designed for about 0.75 m/s minimum (self-cleansing) and around 2.5–3 m/s maximum. Check the project spec.

RTD (IEC 60751)

Callendar–Van Dusen, α = 0.00385. Add lead resistance for 2-wire connections.
